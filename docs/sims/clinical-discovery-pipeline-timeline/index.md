## About This MicroSim

This interactive timeline lays out the nine phases of a graph + AI clinical
discovery pipeline, from aggregating EHR, claims, pharmacy, and lab data into
unified patient graphs through to peer-reviewed publication. Phases are
color-coded by stage — data preparation (blue), discovery and analysis (green),
validation (orange), and dissemination (purple) — and an annotation banner
contrasts the traditional 24–36 month research cycle with the 12–18 month
graph + AI approach, roughly a 40% reduction in time-to-discovery.

## How to Use

Hover over any phase to read its core activity, and click a phase to see a
concrete heart-failure research example unfold in the panel below the timeline.
Use the **Filter** dropdown to isolate a single stage, the **Zoom +/-** buttons
to change the time scale, and **Fit All** to return to the full pipeline view.
