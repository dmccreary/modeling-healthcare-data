## About This MicroSim

This MicroSim plots a network of primary-care providers as a quadrant scatter chart so students can spot high and low performers across multiple metrics — the kind of multi-source view a graph database assembles by aggregating satisfaction, quality, cost, and utilization data per provider. Each provider is a circle positioned by two selectable metrics, sized by patient panel, and colored by quadrant: green for high on both, red for low on both, and amber for mixed.

## How to Use

Use the X-axis and Y-axis dropdowns to choose which two metrics to compare (patient satisfaction, clinical quality, or cost efficiency); the quadrant lines redraw at the medians. Hover any provider to preview its full profile in the side panel, and click to lock it there — revealing satisfaction, quality, cost efficiency, panel size, appointment availability, no-show rate, and years in practice. Click empty space or Reset view to release the selection.
