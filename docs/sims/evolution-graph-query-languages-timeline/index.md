## About This MicroSim

This interactive timeline traces the evolution of graph query languages from
2002 to 2025 — beginning with RDF and SPARQL for the semantic web, through the
industry innovation era of Cypher, GSQL, and openCypher, into the ISO
standardization of GQL, and on to vendor adoption roadmaps. Milestones are
color-coded by era so learners can see how the field moved from academic triple
stores to a single formally standardized graph query language.

## How to Use

Hover over a milestone for a one-line summary, and click it to read the full
detail in the panel below the timeline. Use the **Filter** dropdown to view a
single era, **Zoom +/-** to change the scale, and **Fit All** to frame every
milestone from 2002 through 2025.
