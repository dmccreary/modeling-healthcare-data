# References: Specialty Care, Surgery, and Remote Monitoring

1. [Hospital Readmission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital_readmission) - Wikipedia - Explains 30-day readmission measurement, risk factors, and penalty programs, providing the clinical and policy background behind this chapter's Readmission Risk node and its comorbidity, length-of-stay, and disposition inputs.

2. [Remote Patient Monitoring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_patient_monitoring) - Wikipedia - Covers how connected devices transmit physiologic data to clinicians, directly grounding the chapter's Wearable Device Data, Remote Patient Monitoring, and Telehealth Visit graph pattern in real-world clinical practice.

3. [Palliative Care](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palliative_care) - Wikipedia - Defines specialized comfort- and quality-of-life-focused care for serious illness, supporting the chapter's treatment of Palliative Care as a parallel, non-diagnostic care model distinct from behavioral health screening pathways.

4. Applied Logistic Regression (3rd Edition) - David W. Hosmer Jr., Stanley Lemeshow, and Rodney X. Sturdivant - Wiley - Credited for the Hosmer-Lemeshow goodness-of-fit test, the standard calibration check clinicians use when validating the kind of multi-factor readmission-risk scoring models this chapter introduces.

5. Oxford Textbook of Palliative Medicine (6th Edition) - Nathan I. Cherny, Marie T. Fallon, Stein Kaasa, Russell K. Portenoy, and David C. Currow - Oxford University Press - The consensus text credited with establishing palliative medicine as a distinct discipline, framing the goals-of-care approach this chapter models as separate from disease-focused diagnosis.

6. [Hospital Readmissions](https://www.ahrq.gov/topics/hospital-readmissions.html) - Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality - AHRQ's overview of readmission measurement and reduction strategies, including the Re-Engineered Discharge approach, illustrating the real operational stakes behind the chapter's Care Transition and Readmission Risk nodes.

7. [Remote Patient Monitoring](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/telehealth/remote-patient-monitoring) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services - Official CMS guidance on Medicare's remote physiologic monitoring benefit, detailing device requirements and billable components that mirror the chapter's Wearable Device Data and Remote Patient Monitoring graph relationships.

8. [PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9) Calculator](https://www.mdcalc.com/calc/1725/phq9-patient-health-questionnaire9) - MDCalc - Presents the validated nine-item depression screening instrument and its scoring thresholds, the exact standardized tool this chapter cites as an example of a Mental Health Screening node feeding a Behavioral Health Condition.

9. [Patient Portals: An Online Tool for Your Health](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000880.htm) - MedlinePlus (National Library of Medicine) - A plain-language government explainer of how patients use portals to message providers, view results, and manage records, matching the chapter's description of the Patient Portal as an aggregating node.

10. [Final Rule: 42 CFR Part 2, Confidentiality of Substance Use Disorder Patient Records](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/practice-management/hipaa/42-cfr-part-2) - American Psychiatric Association - Summarizes the federal confidentiality protections this chapter cites as the reason Substance Use Disorder nodes require additional access governance beyond standard condition data.
