# References: Healthcare Financial Forecasting and Risk

1. [Bundled Payment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundled_payment) - Wikipedia - Explains the single fixed-price episode-of-care reimbursement model this chapter uses to show how bundled payments shift complication risk from payer to provider, in contrast to fee-for-service.

2. [Accountable Care Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accountable_care_organization) - Wikipedia - Covers the coordinated provider group structure underlying this chapter's discussion of shared savings programs, one-sided versus two-sided risk, and spending benchmarks.

3. [Total Cost of Ownership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_cost_of_ownership) - Wikipedia - Defines the direct-and-indirect-cost accounting concept this chapter applies to its graph-database migration worked example alongside ROI and break-even analysis.

4. Gapenski's Healthcare Finance: An Introduction to Accounting and Financial Management (7th Edition) - Louis C. Gapenski, Kristin L. Reiter, and Paula H. Song - Health Administration Press - The standard health-administration text credited for its capital investment decision framework, mirroring this chapter's worked TCO, ROI, and break-even analysis of a health system technology investment.

5. Essentials of Managed Health Care (6th Edition) - Peter R. Kongstvedt - Jones & Bartlett Learning - The most widely used managed-care textbook, credited for its detailed treatment of risk pools, capitation arrangements, and ACO shared-savings mechanics that this chapter models as financial risk-sharing structures.

6. [BPCI Advanced](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/innovation-models/bpci-advanced) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services - CMS's official page on its bundled-payment innovation model, describing the 90-day clinical episode and downside risk structure this chapter uses to explain bundled payment incentives.

7. [Shared Savings Program](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-for-service-providers/shared-savings-program-ssp-acos) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services - The official CMS program page explaining how ACOs share in savings against a spending benchmark, directly underlying this chapter's one-sided versus two-sided risk discussion.

8. [7 KPIs Providers Should Be Tracking](https://www.hfma.org/revenue-cycle/kpis/7-kpis-providers-should-be-tracking/) - Healthcare Financial Management Association - An industry benchmark article covering denial rate, days in accounts receivable, and net collection rate thresholds that this chapter cites as revenue-cycle risk indicators.

9. [Data Mine: Measuring Success - Finding the Right Metrics to Optimize the Revenue Cycle](https://www.mgma.com/articles/data-mine-measuring-success-finding-the-right-metrics-to-optimize-the-revenue-cycle) - Medical Group Management Association - Benchmarks accounts receivable aging, days in A/R, and collection percentage against MGMA's Better Performer data, supporting this chapter's net collection rate and Days in AR worked examples.

10. [Break-Even Analysis: How to Calculate the Break-Even Point](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/break-even-analysis/) - Corporate Finance Institute - Walks through the break-even formula and worked example that this chapter applies to determine when a health system's cost-containment technology investment pays for itself.
