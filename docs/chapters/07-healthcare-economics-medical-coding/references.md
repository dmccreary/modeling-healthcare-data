# References: Healthcare Economics and Medical Coding Systems

1. [ICD-10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICD-10) - Wikipedia - Explains the WHO's ICD-10 classification and its expansion into the 70,000-code U.S. ICD-10-CM Clinical Modification, including the category-plus-decimal-specifier structure this chapter decodes for E11.9, I10, and I21.3.

2. [Current Procedural Terminology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Procedural_Terminology) - Wikipedia - Describes the AMA-maintained CPT code set's six Category I sections, annual October update cycle, and licensing model, supporting this chapter's CPT 99213 office-visit billing and RVU productivity examples.

3. [Health care in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_care_in_the_United_States) - Wikipedia - Documents U.S. per-capita and GDP-share healthcare spending, the employer/Medicare/Medicaid payer mix, and integrated delivery systems, grounding this chapter's cost-comparison and payer-provider-patient stakeholder sections.

4. Health Economics: Fundamentals and Flow of Funds (4th Edition) - Thomas E. Getzen - John Wiley & Sons - Getzen originated the "flow of funds" diagram tracing money among patients, providers, payers, employers, and government, a framework widely adopted for teaching the payer-provider-patient triangle this chapter's premium and claims worked example illustrates.

5. Buck's Step-by-Step Medical Coding (2023 Edition) - Carol J. Buck - Elsevier - Buck pioneered the "step-by-step" instructional method with distinctive two-tone highlighting for teaching how to look up and assign ICD, CPT, and HCPCS codes, the standard pedagogy behind AAPC- and AHIMA-aligned coding courses.

6. [ICD-10 | CMS](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/icd-10-codes) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services - Official page listing ICD-10-CM/PCS code files, coding guidelines, and the CDC's role in maintaining diagnosis codes, confirming this chapter's claim that ICD-10-CM updates on an annual schedule.

7. [Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) | CMS](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system) - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services - Explains that HCPCS Level I is identical to CPT while Level II covers durable medical equipment, ambulance transport, and supplies, matching this chapter's E0601 CPAP-device example exactly.

8. [Electronic Health Records and Their Benefits](https://www.healthit.gov/health-it-basics/benefits-ehrs/) - HealthIT.gov (Office of the National Coordinator for Health IT) - Defines the EHR as a longitudinal record spanning multiple care settings and stresses interoperability, reinforcing this chapter's description of the EHR as an integration point across lab, pharmacy, and ordering systems.

9. [National Drug Code Directory | FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/national-drug-code-directory) - U.S. Food and Drug Administration - The FDA's searchable, daily-updated directory of NDC drug identifiers, the authoritative source behind this chapter's labeler-product-package NDC example and its warning about inconsistent zero-padding across systems.

10. [How does health spending in the U.S. compare to other countries?](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/chart-collection/health-spending-u-s-compare-countries/) - Peterson-KFF Health System Tracker - Interactive chart collection comparing U.S. per-person healthcare spending and GDP share against peer nations, the same OECD-style comparison this chapter's opening cost section and MicroSim chart present.
