# References: Clinical Guidelines, Care Pathways, and Provider Workforce

1. [Evidence-Based Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence-based_medicine) - Wikipedia - Explains grounding clinical decisions in published research rather than tradition alone, the foundation this chapter builds on when it chains Clinical Guideline, Best Practice, Care Pathway, and Clinical Protocol into one evidentiary hierarchy.

2. [Clinical Pathway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_pathway) - Wikipedia - Describes multidisciplinary, sequenced treatment plans for specific conditions, directly supporting the chapter's Care Pathway and Clinical Pathway Variance concepts and the ED chest-pain protocol worked example.

3. [Medical Malpractice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_malpractice) - Wikipedia - Covers the legal and clinical basis for malpractice claims, background for the chapter's Malpractice Risk score and its prior-claims, specialty-risk, and case-mix-complexity graph inputs.

4. Evidence-Based Medicine: How to Practice and Teach EBM (Latest Edition) - David L. Sackett, Sharon E. Straus, W. Scott Richardson, William Rosenberg, and R. Brian Haynes - Elsevier / Churchill Livingstone - Sackett is widely credited as the founder of evidence-based medicine, and this book is recognized as the field's founding, best-known introduction, framing the guideline-to-protocol hierarchy this chapter teaches.

5. Case Management: A Practical Guide for Education and Practice (4th Edition) - Suzanne K. Powell and Hussein M. Tahan - Wolters Kluwer - Regarded as the standard reference text for case management practice and certification exam preparation, credited for the coordinating-role framework this chapter uses to distinguish Case Manager from Care Manager.

6. [State Practice Environment](https://www.aanp.org/advocacy/state/state-practice-environment) - American Association of Nurse Practitioners - AANP's official map and definitions of full, reduced, and restricted practice authority, the source for this chapter's discussion of state-by-state Nurse Practitioner prescribing and supervision requirements.

7. [PA Scope of Practice Issue Brief](https://www.aapa.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Issue-brief_Scope-of-Practice_0117-1.pdf) - American Academy of Physician Associates (AAPA) - Lays out the elements of state Physician Assistant practice acts, including supervision and prescriptive authority, matching the chapter's SUPERVISED_BY edge modeling for the Physician Assistant role.

8. [Scope of Practice Overview](https://ccmcertification.org/about-ccmc/about-case-management/scope-practice-overview) - Commission for Case Manager Certification (CCMC) - The certifying body's definition of case management as an assess-plan-coordinate-monitor process, reinforcing the chapter's MANAGES_CASE_FOR edge and its distinction from a specialist's CONSULT_FOR role.

9. [National Practitioner Data Bank](https://www.npdb.hrsa.gov/) - Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) - The federal repository of medical malpractice payments and adverse actions that the chapter cites as the real-world source for a provider's FILED_CLAIM_AGAINST edge count feeding the Malpractice Risk score.

10. [ECRI Guidelines Trust](https://home.ecri.org/pages/ecri-guidelines-trust) - ECRI Institute - A curated, criteria-scored repository of trustworthy clinical practice guidelines that succeeded the AHRQ National Guideline Clearinghouse, illustrating how real Clinical Guideline nodes are sourced, vetted, and kept current.
